# Code of conduct

## Expected behavior

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss the work, not the person's
identity, background, experience, or motives. Give specific technical feedback,
accept corrections, and stop behavior when someone says it is unwelcome.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual attention, deliberate
intimidation, stalking, doxxing, and publication of private information are not
acceptable in project spaces.

## Project spaces

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict,
or ban a participant whose behavior violates this policy. They should consider
context, impact, repeated behavior, and the safety of people involved.

For abuse hosted on GitHub, use GitHub's private report or block tools. The
project does not yet publish a separate private conduct address. Do not place
sensitive conduct reports or personal information in a public issue. A private
project contact should be added before broad community launch.
